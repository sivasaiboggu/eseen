Welcome to Eseen

Eseen adds an invisible one-pixel image to the emails you choose to track in Gmail, and shows you when that image is loaded.

What Eseen does

  • Registers a tracked send when you send with tracking on.
  • Shows a per-send status: sent, opened, or possibly opened.
  • Optionally notifies you on first open.

What it cannot tell you

  • A status describes an image load, never proof anyone read your email.
  • Blocked images can hide real opens; automatic prefetch (for example by Apple Mail or security scanners) can cause false opens. Eseen marks these "possibly opened" when it can recognize them.
  • Eseen reads only the recipient fields of emails you send with tracking on, the subject line only if you opt in, and Gmail thread identifiers used to place status badges. Email bodies and the rest of your inbox are never read, and none of that content leaves Gmail.

Your data

  • Recipient addresses are stored as salted hashes. Subject lines are stored only if you opt in, and then encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) in the production deployment, where the project owner configures the data key.
  • No ads, no selling data. You can export or delete all your data at any time from the dashboard.
  • Recipients can opt out at the opt-out page; opted-out recipients' opens are no longer recorded for you.

You can review permissions and privacy details anytime from the extension's settings.
